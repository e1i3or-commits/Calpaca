# Code of Conduct

Calpaca's maintainers and contributors pledge to make participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or
identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive technical feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report incidents privately through
[GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/e1i3or-commits/Calpaca/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, and temporarily or
permanently ban contributors who do not follow this code.

This code is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
